[Kliper](https://kliper.dev/) — multi-tenant B2B SaaS for PCI DSS v4.0.1 compliance, the workspace QSA firms use to run assessments and produce the Report on Compliance. TypeScript, Next.js, React, Express, PostgreSQL/Prisma and Redis, on self-managed Linux infrastructure.

- **Zero to production in 10 months** — a 102-table relational domain model, REST API, 700+ component front end, 4-locale i18n and technical documentation: 2,100+ commits and 620+ issues delivered.

- **Authentication & identity** — cookie-based sessions, TOTP MFA, WebAuthn passkeys, enterprise SSO over OIDC, org-scoped RBAC and per-tenant 2FA enforcement.

- **Application security ownership** — multi-tenant isolation, CSRF and SSRF controls, rate limiting, secrets management, malware scanning and audit logging. Ran recurring security reviews, drove remediation to closure, and rotated exposed credentials with documented residual risk.

- **Infrastructure & SRE** — 6 VMs across production and staging behind Nginx, pm2 clustering, zero-downtime deploys, GitHub Actions CI, release tagging and rollback. Point-in-time recovery to object storage with sub-minute RPO and weekly restore drills; zero-trust staging with no public ingress.

- **Trust posture** — DNSSEC, CAA, HSTS, TLS hardening, security.txt, a public status page, a SOC 2 readiness programme, and subprocessor and data-residency documentation.

- **AI engineering** — RAG over the firm's own prior assessments with vector search and cited, grounded generation; per-tenant usage metering and cost controls; a strict no-training-on-customer-data boundary.

- **Commercial** — subscription billing, plan gating and entitlements, usage-based limits, free-trial lifecycle, product analytics, and positioning against incumbent GRC tools.

- **Directed AI-assisted development at production scale** — authored the engineering standards, migration workflow, deployment discipline and review gates that kept an AI-built codebase coherent and auditable across 2,100+ commits.
